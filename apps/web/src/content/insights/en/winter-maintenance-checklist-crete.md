The end of the season is the best time to protect your property. A few hours of preparation in October or November can prevent expensive surprises in April. This is the checklist we use as a starting point; every property needs its own version.

## Outside

- **Roofs and terraces:** clear drains, gutters and terrace outlets of leaves and sand. Blocked drains are the most common cause of winter leaks.
- **Pergolas, awnings and umbrellas:** take down or secure everything that can catch the wind.
- **Outdoor furniture and cushions:** clean, dry and store under cover.
- **Garden:** prune, check irrigation timers and reduce watering for winter.
- **External lights and sockets:** check covers and seals against rain.

## Pool

- Decide whether the pool stays open or is winterised. Either way, balance the water chemistry first.
- Lower the water level if required for your system, clean filters and protect the pump and equipment from rain.
- Fit a secure cover where appropriate, and schedule periodic checks.

## Inside

- **Humidity:** closed houses near the sea get damp. Leave interior doors open, use moisture absorbers or dehumidifiers, and plan regular airing visits.
- **Water:** turn off the main supply if the property will be empty, or at least the water heater. Check for drips.
- **Electrics:** switch off non-essential circuits, but keep essentials such as security systems running.
- **Kitchen:** empty and clean the fridge and leave it slightly open, and remove anything that could attract insects.
- **Textiles:** wash and store linen and towels in sealed, dry storage.

## Systems

- Service air-conditioning units before or after the season, cleaning filters and checking drainage.
- Note any appliances that need repair or replacement, so they can be ordered in the quiet months.
- Test smoke detectors and check fire extinguishers' service dates.

## Security

- Lock and check all shutters, windows and gates.
- Make sure someone local holds keys and can access the property after a storm.
- Inform your insurer if required by your policy for unoccupied periods.

## During the winter

Schedule regular visits, more often after storms or heavy rain. Each visit should check for leaks, humidity, damage and security, with photos sent to the owner.

## Use the quiet months well

Winter is the time for repainting, repairs, upgrades and deep cleaning, when contractors have more availability. Plan them in November and the property will open in spring in better shape than it closed.
