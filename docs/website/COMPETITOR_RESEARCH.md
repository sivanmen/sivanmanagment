## What we adopted
- **Long, honest FAQ** on every service page (ARENCOS) — 6 Q&As each, plus a 17-question FAQ page and FAQPage schema.
- **Audience segmentation** (Domicile) — "Who it is for" block on every service page; owners abroad emphasised.
- **One-line service explanations** (Bluebell) — `short` text in the mega-menu and cards.
- **Process visual** (Domicile) — 7-step A–Z timeline with scroll progress.
- **Assessment-first conversion** (Bluebell/Mint) — "Request a Property Assessment" everywhere; 4-step qualifying form.

## What we deliberately did NOT copy
- Income uplift percentages and income guarantees (ARENCOS) — the brief forbids unverifiable or guaranteed results.
- Client counts, review stars, "award-winning" — nothing verified yet.
- "Free" evaluation as a headline promise — the owner has not confirmed pricing policy (wording: "without obligation").

## Differentiation (used across the copy)
1. **Multi-asset under one roof:** residential + vacation + commercial + hospitality + maintenance + representation + marketing/sales support. Competitors are mostly short-term-rental specialists.
2. **Own technology:** Sivan Management runs its own PMS with an online **Owner Portal** (`client.sivanmanagment.com`) — bookings, statements, maintenance work orders, documents. This is real (see `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md`) and shown on the site as a labelled illustration.
3. **Office in central Chania** (Plateia Eleftherias 2) — verifiable local presence.
4. **Owner-first transparency:** written scope, expense-approval limits, itemised statements, no guarantees.
5. **Eight languages incl. Hebrew, Chinese and Russian** — wider than any competitor reviewed.
